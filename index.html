FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no ads.

Do I have to give Puffium my card details?

No, and it'll never ask. When you check out, Amazon or Target uses the card already saved in your account. Puffium never sees your card number or passwords.

Will it place orders on its own?

No. Puffium can put an item in your cart and open checkout, but you always press the final “Place order” button yourself.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC” when I open it.

Windows shows that for new apps from small developers that haven't paid for a code-signing certificate. Click More info → Run anyway. You only need to do this once.

Does it need to stay open?

It needs to be running to watch, but closing the window doesn't stop it. It keeps going quietly in the system tray by your clock. Right-click the tray icon and choose Exit to quit completely.

Where is my data kept?

On your computer only, in %AppData%\Puffium. Puffium talks only to the stores you watch and to your Discord channel if you set one up.

Does it work on Mac or my phone?

Puffium is for Windows 10 and 11. To get alerts on your phone, connect a Discord channel and turn on Discord notifications.
